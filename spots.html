Local Spot Guide

Here's our running list of the best spots in town. Remember to skate respectfully, watch out for security, and never skate a spot that's clearly posted no-skateboarding unless you're cool with bailing fast.

Street Spots

  • Downtown Ledges - smooth ground, mellow ledges, watch for security around lunch
  • The Big Gap - by the old warehouse, only for the brave
  • School Handrails - best on weekends when campus is empty
  • Bank Plaza - great banks but gets crowded after school

Ranked: Best Skate Parks Nearby

  1. Riverside Skatepark - best bowl in the area, always packed on weekends
  2. Community Park - good for beginners, chill vibe
  3. Old Mill Park - rough concrete but a fun old-school layout

Not sure how to find a legit skatepark near you? The Skateparks.com directory has a solid list you can search by location.
