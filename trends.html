What's Hot Right Now

Every month the crew rounds up what's actually happening in the scene, from board graphics to trick trends to what everyone's wearing at the park. Here's the latest.

Gear Trends

  • Baggy cargo pants are back in a big way
  • Chunky skate shoes with thick soles for better board feel
  • Trucker hats worn backwards, obviously
  • Griptape art getting way more creative lately

Trick Trends

  1. Switch flip tricks are everywhere right now
  2. Big spin variations making a comeback
  3. Manual combos getting longer and more technical
  4. Bigger gaps being sessioned by the local crew

Curious how skate fashion has evolved over the years? There's a great breakdown of skateboarding culture and style history on Wikipedia if you wanna go down that rabbit hole.
