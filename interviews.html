Interview of the Month: "Sk8rBoy_2003"

We caught up with local legend Sk8rBoy_2003 after his gnarly session at the downtown bowl. Here's what he had to say about the scene, his gear, and where he sees skating headed next.

GNARLY.NET: So how long you been skating?
Sk8rBoy_2003: Since I was like 9. My older cousin gave me his old board and I just never stopped after that.

GNARLY.NET: What's your favorite trick right now?
Sk8rBoy_2003: Probably switch heelflips. Took me forever to land one clean but once you get it, it feels so good.

GNARLY.NET: Any advice for beginners?
Sk8rBoy_2003: Just skate as much as you can and don't worry about looking dumb. Everyone slams. That's part of it.
